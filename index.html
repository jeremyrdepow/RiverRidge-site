Overview

Independent Reading of a Changing Environment

Government and institutional decisions shape risk and opportunity long before they become public and implemented. My work is to provide a clear read of what is happening across policy, regulation, and politics, drawn from current engagement and years in the policy environment. I focus on what that means for the decisions and the business strategy in front of you.

My work is analytic, without agenda or house view. A thoughtful read of the environment, grounded in fact and in dialogue with the people who actually shape the outcome.

About

Senior Experience, Genuine Independence

Jeremy R. Depow

Principal · RiverRidge Policy & Risk Intelligence

Twenty-five years of work at the intersection of technological innovation and public policy, beginning on Parliament Hill and running from installing internet-connected environmental weather stations to executive roles in digital policy. Along the way: government relations at Intuit, senior policy work at Pfizer, founding Canada's Digital Policy Forum, executive and board-level roles with think tanks and not-for-profits across the biopharmaceutical and information technology sectors, and corporate policy at a global IT services company. Selected to the U.S. State Department's International Visitor Leadership Program on cybersecurity in 2018.

RiverRidge is the practice through which I take on that work.

That range of vantage points is the foundation of my practice. Having had the opportunity to see how policy is made from inside government, inside major companies, and inside civil society, I look at files for what is actually driving them, and for the detail that sits well beyond the press release.

I am also a volunteer firefighter and a ground search and rescue team leader, trained in the Incident Command System. The privilege of doing that work has grounded my approach in how decisions play out in real life, under stress and under risk, and not only how they read on paper.

The practice is deliberately independent. My aim is not to lobby or carry a house view, but to provide the best information and analysis available, aligned only with a client's need.

How I Work

Every engagement combines analytic tradecraft with verifiable evidence and direct sources:

  • Policy and regulatory landscape analysis
  • Political and institutional risk assessment
  • Primary-source insight from people close to the file
  • Scenario and second-order risk evaluation

I help people understand trade-offs, exposure, and constraints, and in doing so protect capital, resources, and reputation. The goal is evidence-based clarity.

Independence by Design

The practice may support a client's position, but it does not lobby or carry a house view. That independence maintains credibility with stakeholders and keeps the analysis aligned with the client's decision.
