SECU Committee Meeting

Notices of Meeting include information about the subject matter to be examined by the committee and date, time and place of the meeting, as well as a list of any witnesses scheduled to appear. The Evidence is the edited and revised transcript of what is said before a committee. The Minutes of Proceedings are the official record of the business conducted by the committee at a sitting.

For an advanced search, use Publication Search tool.

If you have any questions or comments regarding the accessibility of this publication, please contact us at accessible@parl.gc.ca.

Standing Committee on Public Safety and National Security (SECU)
45th Parliament, 1st Session
Meeting 36
Tuesday, May 5, 2026, 3:56 p.m. to 5:53 p.m.
Room 415, Wellington Building, 197 Sparks Street
Televised
Presiding
Hon. Jean-Yves Duclos, Chair (Liberal)

Bill C-22, An Act respecting lawful access
Appearing

Witnesses

• Sébastien Aubertin-Giguère, Associate Assistant Deputy Minister, National and Cyber Security Branch
• Shannon Hiegel, Director general, National Security Policy Directorate
• Mike McGuire, Director General, International and Border Policy
• Fenton Ho, Director, Intelligence Policy
Royal Canadian Mounted Police
• D/Commr Marie-Claude Dandenault, Deputy Commissioner, National Specialized Services
• C/Supt Richard Burchill, Director General, Technical Investigation Services
Pursuant to the order of reference of the House of Monday, April 20, 2026, and the motion adopted by the committee on Thursday, April 30, 2026, the committee commenced its study of Bill C-22, An Act respecting lawful access.

Mike McGuire made a statement and, with Richard Burchill, Kimberly Gibner, Shannon Hiegel, Fenton Ho, Anne‑Marie LeBel, Ramzi Nashef, answered questions.

At 4:52 p.m., the meeting was suspended.

At 4:57 p.m., the meeting resumed.

The Minister of Public Safety and the Minister of Justice made statements and, with Nicole Giles, answered questions.

At 5:53 p.m., the committee adjourned to the call of the Chair.



Paul Cardegna
Clerk of the committee
