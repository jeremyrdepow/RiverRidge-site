House Publications

The Debates are the report—transcribed, edited, and corrected—of what is said in the House. The Journals are the official record of the decisions and other transactions of the House. The Order Paper and Notice Paper contains the listing of all items that may be brought forward on a particular sitting day, and notices for upcoming items.

For an advanced search, use Publication Search tool.

If you have any questions or comments regarding the accessibility of this publication, please contact us at accessible@parl.gc.ca.

Questions
Q-12582 — June 4, 2026 — Cathay Wagantall (Yorkton—Melville) — With regard to Veterans Affairs Canada contracting Partners in Canadian Veterans Rehabilitation Services to provide rehabilitation services to veterans, since the contract began: (a) how many veterans have been referred by Veterans Affairs Canada to Partners in Canadian Veterans Rehabilitation Services, broken down by fiscal year; (b) how many referrals resulted in veterans receiving rehabilitation or related services through Partners in Canadian Veterans Rehabilitation Services, broken down by fiscal year; (c) what is the total amount paid by Veterans Affairs Canada to Partners in Canadian Veterans Rehabilitation Services since inception, broken down by fiscal year; (d) what are the details of all contracts between Veterans Affairs Canada and Partners in Canadian Veterans Rehabilitation Services, including, for each, the (i) date, (ii) initial value, (iii) current value, if different from the initial value, (iv) duration, (v) description of the services provided; and (e) what is the average and total cost per veteran served through Partners in Canadian Veterans Rehabilitation Services, broken down by fiscal year?
Q-12592 — June 4, 2026 — Alex Ruff (Bruce—Grey—Owen Sound) — With regard to ongoing trade negotiations between Canada and Mercosur member countries and the potential impact on Canada’s beef sector: (a) what are the details of all consultations that the government has held since December 12, 2025, with Canadian beef producers, provincial cattle organizations, national agricultural organizations, industry associations, or other interested parties, in which concerns related to Mercosur beef imports, beef market access, or impacts on the Canadian beef sector were raised, including, for each, (i) the date, (ii) who conducted the consultation, (iii) the names and titles of participants and organizations, (iv) which concerns were raised and by which organizations, (v) the government’s response to each concern; (b) what are the details of all consultations that the government has held since December 12, 2025, with Canadian beef producers, provincial cattle organizations, national agricultural organizations, industry associations, or other interested parties in which support was expressed for increased trade with Mercosur countries or for expanded beef market access under a Canada-Mercosur trade agreement, including, for each, (i) the date, (ii) who conducted the consultation, (iii) the names and titles of participants and organizations, (iv) the reasons stated for support, broken down by each organization that expressed support; (c) what are the details of all written submissions or correspondence received by the government related to Mercosur negotiations, since December 12, 2025, including, for each, the (i) organization represented, (ii) date received, (iii) title, (iv) summary of the contents, including any concerns raised or supportive statements, (v) type of document; (d) what are the details, including methodology, dates, and findings of any projections, forecasts, economic models, impact assessments, or other analyses that the government has completed since December 12, 2025, regarding (i) the potential impact of Mercosur beef imports on Canadian cattle prices, farm revenues, herd expansion, domestic processing capacity, or Canadian market share, (ii) the expected benefits of a Canada-Mercosur trade agreement for Canadian farmers, ranchers, processors, or agricultural exporters; (e) what are the details of all assessments, analyses, briefing materials, scientific evaluations, or risk evaluations related to cattle diseases like Foot-and-mouth disease, animal health standards, biosecurity measures, sanitary and phytosanitary measures, or traceability systems in Mercosur countries which have been prepared since December 12, 2025, including, for each, the (i) title, (ii) completion date, (iii) country or countries examined, (iv) risk area examined, (v) findings or conclusions; (f) what analysis has the government completed since December 12, 2025, regarding compensation, transition supports, safeguard measures, or other mitigation measures for Canadian beef producers related to any potential increase in beef imports from Mercosur countries, including, for each, (i) the title, (ii) the completion date, (iii) the measure analyzed, (iv) who conducted the analysis, (v) the findings or conclusions; and (g) what are the details of any contracts signed related to any of the consultations or analyses conducted on this topic, including, for each, the (i) date, (ii) vendor, (iii) value, (iv) description of the goods or services provided?
Q-12602 — June 4, 2026 — Marilène Gill (Côte-Nord—Kawawachikamach—Nitassinan) — With regard to Employment Insurance sickness benefit claimants: (a) have investigative guidelines been issued to specifically target sickness benefit claimants; (b) if so, what are these guidelines; (c) have investigative guidelines been issued to specifically target sickness benefit claimants in the Côte-Nord administrative region; (d) if so, what are these guidelines; (e) how many investigations specific to Employment Insurance sickness benefit claimants residing on the North Shore are currently underway; and (f) how many investigations specifically targeting Employment Insurance sickness benefit claimants residing on the North Shore were conducted between June 2025 and June 2026?
