House Publications

The Debates are the report—transcribed, edited, and corrected—of what is said in the House. The Journals are the official record of the decisions and other transactions of the House. The Order Paper and Notice Paper contains the listing of all items that may be brought forward on a particular sitting day, and notices for upcoming items.

For an advanced search, use Publication Search tool.

If you have any questions or comments regarding the accessibility of this publication, please contact us at accessible@parl.gc.ca.

Introduction of Government Bills
June 8, 2026 — The Minister of Canadian Identity and Culture and Minister responsible for Official Languages — Bill entitled “An Act to enact the Digital Safety Act and the Digital Safety Commission of Canada Act and to make consequential amendments to other Acts”.
Recommendation
(Pursuant to Standing Order 79(2))
Her Excellency the Governor General recommends to the House of Commons the appropriation of public revenue under the circumstances, in the manner and for the purposes set out in a measure entitled “An Act to enact the Digital Safety Act and the Digital Safety Commission of Canada Act and to make consequential amendments to other Acts”.

Questions
Q-12662 — June 8, 2026 — The Honourable Andrew Scheer (Regina—Qu'Appelle) — With regard to the government's high-speed rail project: (a) what are the details of all contracts with a value over $100,000 signed by the government, VIA Rail or Alto in relation to the project including, for each, the (i) date, (ii) vendor, (iii) value, (iv) duration, (v) description of the goods or services, (vi) manner in which the contract was awarded (sole-sourced or competitive bid); (b) which of the contracts in (a) include clauses related to financial liability or cancellation penalties should the government choose not to proceed with the high-speed rail project, and what are the details, including the vendors and amounts associated with those clauses; and (c) what are the details of any financial liabilities, required payments or cancellation penalties the current government has agreed to pay should the high-speed rail project not proceed or come to fruition?
Q-12672 — June 8, 2026 — Bob Zimmer (Prince George—Peace River—Northern Rockies) — With regard to the Assault Style Firearms Compensation Program and the related Amnesty Order: (a) when the program began, how many individuals does the government estimate were in possession of impacted firearms; (b) how many individuals have communicated an intent to comply by October 30, 2026, by (i) turning in a firearm for compensation, (ii) rendering the firearm inoperable, (iii) otherwise disposing of the firearm; (c) for those who have not yet communicated an intent to comply, has the government undertaken plans to increase compliance, including further incentives to comply, an extension of the amnesty period to encourage compliance or enforcement actions against those who are non-compliant; and (d) has the National Weapons Enforcement Support Team communicated with Chief Firearms Officers or police of local jurisdiction to identify targets for enforcement action, and, if so, what are the details, including the dates of all such communication?
Q-12682 — June 8, 2026 — Kelly Block (Carlton Trail—Eagle Creek) — With regard to all reinvestments of sales, royalties and revenues by the government noted in the Supplementary Estimates (A), 2026-27: what is the detailed and itemized breakdown of how that money was reinvested, including, for each expenditure, the Treasury Board object code associated with each expenditure and the associated amounts?
Q-12692 — June 8, 2026 — Kelly Block (Carlton Trail—Eagle Creek) — With regard to the funding for the Canada Housing Benefit for Survivors of Gender-Based Violence: (a) since the inception of the program, how many survivors or victims have received funding or housing support under the program, broken down by year and province or territory; (b) how much funding was distributed under the program since inception, broken down by year and province or territory; (c) what is the average and maximum amount of support provided per recipient; (d) how many survivors or victims are projected to receive support from the additional $7,891,853 which is in the Supplementary Estimates (A) 2026-27; and (e) what criteria or eligibility requirements are used to determine who qualifies for support under the program?
Q-12702 — June 8, 2026 — Rob Morrison (Columbia—Kootenay—Southern Rockies) — With regard to the $736.8 million in funding provided to the Canadian Air Transport Security Authority, referenced in the Supplementary Estimates (A), 2026-27, for the replacement and upgrade of screening equipment, including computed tomography scanners: (a) how many contracts have been signed related to the acquisition, installation, maintenance or support of the screening equipment with the $736.8 million; (b) what are the details of each contract, including the (i) vendor name, (ii) contract number, (iii) contract value, (iv) date the contract was awarded, (v) description of the goods or services provided, (vi) manner in which the contract was awarded (sole-sourced or competitive bid); (c) how many scanners or units have been (i) purchased, (ii) delivered, to date from the funding, in total and broken down by vendor; (d) in which country was each scanner or unit manufactured; and (e) to which airports or facilities have the scanners or units been delivered or are planned to be delivered?
Q-12712 — June 8, 2026 — Tony Baldinelli (Niagara Falls—Niagara-on-the-Lake) — With regard to the government's Indigenous Tourism Fund's Signature Indigenous Tourism Experiences Stream, broken down by year, for each of the last five fiscal years: (a) how much funding has the government provided for the stream; (b) how much did the government pay the National Aboriginal Capital Corporations Association to administer the stream; (c) what was the formal application process for indigenous tourism businesses to access this funding, and what were the targets, including timelines associated with the application process; and (d) what are the details of all stream funding recipients, including, for each, the (i) recipient, (ii) amount provided, (iii) date, (iv) type of funding (grant, repayable loan, etc.), (v) purpose of the funding, (vi) type of tourism business (hotel, tour operator, etc.)?
